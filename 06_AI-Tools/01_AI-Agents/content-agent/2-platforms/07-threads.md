# Threads

## Platform DNA
- Text-first, conversational, opinion-driven
- 500 character limit per post
- Vibe: relaxed, community-feel, more casual than X
- Audience: growing, more [[04-casual]], less tech-heavy

## Content Rules
- Opinions and hot takes perform best
- More conversational than [[01-x]], think "talking out loud"
- No hashtags. no links. pure text engagement
- Match [[02-brand-voice]] at its most relaxed. see [[01-platform-tone]]
- Cross-post adapted versions of [[01-x]] content, but rewrite the hook and soften the tone

---

## Pre-Writing Questions
Answer these before writing:

1. What is the one opinion or observation you want to share?
2. Does this sound like something you'd say out loud to a friend?
3. Is it under 500 characters? (count before posting)
4. Does it invite a reply or reaction — even without asking explicitly?

If it sounds formal or like a "post" — it's not right for Threads. rewrite it as if you're texting a friend.

## Post Templates

### Template A — Hot Take
```
[bold opinion — 1 sentence]

[1-2 lines of reasoning or contrast]

[optional: punchline or open question]
```
Max 500 characters total.

---

### Template B — Shower Thought
```
[casual observation that reframes something obvious]

[why it's actually true — 1-2 lines]
```

---

### Template C — Mini Tip
```
[one actionable thing — under 300 chars]

[optional: one line of why]
```

---

### Template D — Soft Question
```
[share a perspective or experience — 2-3 lines]

[end with an open question — not "what do you think?" but something specific]
```

## Good vs Bad

### ❌ Bad — sounds like a LinkedIn post on Threads
```
"Content marketing is evolving rapidly. AI tools are enabling 
creators to produce more content in less time. Here are 3 
strategies to leverage this trend for your brand. #AI #Content"
```
Why it fails: too formal, hashtags, sounds like it was copy-pasted, no personality.

### ✅ Good — sounds like a real person talking
```
hot take: most people don't need more content.

they need one piece of content that actually says something.

quality still beats quantity. always will.
```
Why it works: casual, opinionated, no hashtags, feels like a real thought not a post.

---

## Formats That Work
1. **Hot take** — bold opinion, 1-3 sentences
2. **Shower thought** — casual observation that makes people think
3. **Question** — invite discussion, ask what your audience thinks
4. **Mini tip** — one actionable thing, under 300 chars
