# Instagram

## Platform DNA
- Caption limit: 2,200 characters, but most engagement comes from the visual (carousel slides or Reels)
- Vibe: visual-first. the image/carousel stops the scroll, the caption closes the deal. bold, clean, designed
- Audience: wider and more [[04-casual]] than X or LinkedIn. more aspirational, more visual learners

## Content Rules
- Carousels are king. 7-10 slides, one idea per slide
- Slide 1 = the hook. bold claim, large text, something contrarian or curiosity-driven. use [[01-hooks]], discovery and proof hooks work best in a visual format
- Match [[02-brand-voice]] but simpler language. see [[01-platform-tone]]
- Captions should add context the slides dont cover. dont just repeat the slides in text form
- Hashtags: 5-10 relevant ones at the end of caption. hashtags actually work on Instagram for discovery
- CTA in the last slide AND caption: "Save this for later" / "Follow for more [topic]" / "Share with someone who needs this"

---

## Pre-Writing Questions
Answer these before writing a single word:

1. What is the ONE big idea this carousel teaches? (one sentence)
2. Can Slide 1 hook be written in 8 words or less?
3. Does each slide have only ONE idea? (if not, split it)
4. What does the reader save or share this for?
5. What is the CTA on the last slide?

If Slide 1 isn't strong enough to stop a scroll — rewrite it before touching any other slide.

## Post Templates

### Template A — How-To Carousel
```
Slide 1: [bold claim — max 8 words]
Slide 2: [problem setup — why this matters]
Slide 3: [step 1 or insight 1]
Slide 4: [step 2 or insight 2]
Slide 5: [step 3 or insight 3]
Slide 6: [step 4 or insight 4]
Slide 7: [the result or transformation]
Slide 8: [biggest mistake to avoid]
Last slide: "Save this. Follow for more [niche]."

Caption: [2-3 sentences expanding on the main idea]
[line break]
[5-10 hashtags]
```

---

### Template B — List Carousel
```
Slide 1: "[N] things that [outcome]" — max 8 words
Slide 2-N: one item per slide — bold headline + 1-line explanation
Last slide: [CTA + your handle]

Caption: [teaser that makes them want to swipe]
[line break]
[5-10 hashtags]
```

---

### Template C — Before/After Carousel
```
Slide 1: "Stop doing [X]. Start doing [Y]."
Slide 2: [Before — the wrong way, 1 idea]
Slide 3: [After — the right way, 1 idea]
Slide 4-7: [repeat for each comparison]
Last slide: "Which one are you? Save this as a reminder."
```

## Good vs Bad

### ❌ Bad — walls of text, no visual hierarchy
```
Slide 1: "5 Tips for Better Content Marketing Strategy in 2024"
Slide 2: "First, you should consider your target audience and 
what kind of content they prefer to consume on a daily basis..."
```
Why it fails: headline too long, slide 2 is a paragraph, nobody reads past slide 1.

### ✅ Good — bold, one idea per slide, makes you swipe
```
Slide 1: "I run 10 accounts. Write nothing manually."
Slide 2: The problem: most people open AI and just start typing.
Slide 3: The fix: give AI a playbook, not just a prompt.
Slide 4: Step 1 — build your brand voice file.
...
```
Why it works: slide 1 stops the scroll, each slide is one thought, you want to swipe to see the next one.

## Carousel Design Rules
- Font: bold, sans-serif, high contrast against background
- One idea per slide. max 30 words per slide
- Consistent color scheme across all slides (your brand colors)
- Slide 1: big bold text, no more than 8 words
- Last slide: clear CTA with your handle
