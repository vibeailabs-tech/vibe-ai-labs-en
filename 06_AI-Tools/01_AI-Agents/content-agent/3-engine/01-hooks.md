# Hook Formulas

Use these to open every post. Match hook type to platform and topic.
See [[01-platform-tone]] for delivery adjustments.

## The Playbook Hook
"Here's [N] steps to [desirable outcome]:"
- Best on: X, LinkedIn, YouTube titles
- Examples:
  - "Here's [N] steps to [your outcome]:" → [YOUR EXAMPLE]
  - "Here's how I [your result] without [common pain point]:" → [YOUR EXAMPLE]

## The Proof Hook
"[Before metric] → [after metric] in [timeframe]"
- Best on: X, LinkedIn
- Examples:
  - "[your before number] → [your after number] in [timeframe]" → [YOUR EXAMPLE]
  - "[old cost/effort] → [new cost/effort] after [what changed]" → [YOUR EXAMPLE]

## The Contrarian Hook
"You don't need [conventional thing]. You need [this instead]."
- Best on: X, Threads
- Examples:
  - "You don't need [common assumption in your niche]." → [YOUR EXAMPLE]
  - "Everyone says [X]. The real answer is [Y]." → [YOUR EXAMPLE]

## The Replacement Hook
"I replaced [expensive/complex thing] with [simple thing]"
- Best on: X, TikTok, Instagram slide 1
- Examples:
  - "I replaced [old way] with [your new way]" → [YOUR EXAMPLE]
  - "I stopped [bad habit in your niche] and did [this] instead" → [YOUR EXAMPLE]

## The Discovery Hook
"I just found [valuable thing]"
- Best on: X
- Examples:
  - "I just found [tool/method] that [result it gives]" → [YOUR EXAMPLE]

## The Behind-the-Scenes Hook
"I run [impressive thing] and [surprising method]"
- Best on: X, LinkedIn, YouTube titles
- Examples:
  - "I [impressive claim about your work] and [surprising how]" → [YOUR EXAMPLE]

## Rules
- Test 2-3 hooks per topic before publishing
- Track which types get the most engagement PER PLATFORM
- Update this weekly. remove underperformers, add new winners
- Hook for [[01-x]] is almost never the same as hook for [[02-linkedin]]
