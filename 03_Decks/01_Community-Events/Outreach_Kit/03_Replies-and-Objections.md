# 03 — Trả lời khi đối tác phản hồi

Dùng chung cho cả hai giọng. Bản dưới viết theo giọng Henry ("I"); khi gửi dưới tên Vibe AI Labs, đổi "I" → "we" và ký "The Vibe AI Labs team".

---

## 1. "Send me more info" / "Gửi thêm thông tin"

```
Of course. Here's the deck: {DECK_LINK}

The short version: we bring the audience (4 communities, 100,000+ members), run a hands-on meetup or workshop, produce Vietnamese onboarding content and send you a report afterwards. Most teams start with a single Meetup Partner event.

What would you most want out of Vietnam: users, feedback, contributors or hires? I'll tailor the proposal to that.
```

## 2. "What numbers can you share?" / "Cho xin số liệu"

Chỉ đưa số thật. Nếu chưa có số người tham dự từng buổi, nói thẳng:

```
Happy to be specific. Our communities have 100,000+ members across 4 groups, which you can check on Facebook directly. For event-level data, we'll share registrations, check-ins, feedback scores and activation through a unique link for your event, all in the post-event report.
```

Khi đã có số liệu meetup thật (từ Luma/Facebook), thêm một câu:
`Our last {topic} meetup had {N registrations} registrations and {N check-ins} check-ins.`

## 3. "How much does it cost?" / "Chi phí bao nhiêu?"

```
It depends on scope: online spotlight only, one meetup, or a 3–6 month program. I'd rather quote something that fits your goal than send a generic price list. If you share what you'd like to achieve and a rough budget range, I'll come back with a concrete proposal within a few days.
```

(Không đưa con số qua tin nhắn khi chưa hiểu phạm vi — đúng nguyên tắc "Báo giá theo phạm vi".)

## 4. "We already have a community / ambassadors"

```
That's great, and it makes this easier. We're not trying to replace your community. Think of us as the local chapter: Vietnamese-language content, in-person sessions in Ho Chi Minh City, and a direct line to builders here who aren't on Discord yet. Happy to work alongside your ambassadors.
```

## 5. "Not a priority right now" / "Để sau"

```
Totally understand. Mind if I check back in {month/quarter}? In the meantime, we'll keep sharing {Project} content with our community, and I'll send you anything interesting we hear from users.
```

Ghi lịch nhắc vào sheet theo dõi.

## 6. "We don't have budget" / "Không có ngân sách"

```
Understood. Two lighter options:
1) Credits instead of cash: if you sponsor trial credits for attendees, we can run a community workshop and cover the rest ourselves.
2) A co-hosted online session (AMA or livestream) with your team in our communities.
Would either work?
```

(Chỉ đề xuất phương án 1 nếu Vibe AI Labs thực sự chấp nhận tự chi phần còn lại.)

## 7. "Can you guarantee X attendees / signups?"

```
I won't promise a number I can't control. What I can commit to: promotion across our 4 communities, a clear registration target we agree on together, and honest reporting on registrations, check-ins and activation afterwards.
```

## 8. "Who else have you worked with?"

```
We've hosted community meetups on AI agents, OpenClaw and Hermes Agent, and we build AI agent solutions for clients, including Hermes Agent setups. I can share photos and agendas from past sessions on a call.
```

(Chỉ nêu tên đối tác/khách hàng khi đã được họ cho phép.)

## 9. Người nhận chuyển cho người khác

```
Thanks {First name}! Hi {New contact}, great to meet you. Short version: we run 4 AI communities in Vietnam with 100,000+ members and would like to organize a {Project} hands-on session here. Deck: {DECK_LINK}. Would a 20-minute call work this week? {CALL_LINK}
```

## 10. Email tóm tắt sau cuộc gọi

**Subject:** {Project} × Vibe AI Labs — recap & next steps

```

Thanks for the time today. Recap of what we discussed:

Goal: {e.g. first 200 active users in Vietnam / feedback on onboarding / find contributors}
Format: {Meetup Partner / Community Spotlight / Ecosystem Partner}

Your side: {speaker, credits, swag, brand assets}
Our side: venue, promotion across our communities, Vietnamese materials, run-of-show, recap content, post-event report

Next steps:
1. We send a scoped proposal by {date}.
2. You confirm speaker and date.
3. We kick off with a brief 4 weeks before the event.

Deck for reference: {DECK_LINK}

Henry
```
