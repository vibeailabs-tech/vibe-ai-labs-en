# Newsletter

## Platform DNA
- Email-based. you own the audience (no algorithm to fight)
- 1,000-2,000 words per issue. deep-dive format
- Vibe: direct, personal. like a letter from a friend who's also your mentor. most personal of all platforms

## Content Rules
- Subject line = your hook. use [[01-hooks]] adapted for email. "The system that replaced my $8k/mo content team" not "Weekly Newsletter #47"
- Open with a story or personal observation, then transition to the tactical stuff
- One core topic per issue. dont try to cover everything
- Match [[02-brand-voice]] at its most personal. see [[01-platform-tone]]
- End with ONE clear CTA. reply to the email, check out a resource, or try something specific
- Plain text or minimal design. fancy templates look like marketing spam. plain text looks like a real person

---

## Pre-Writing Questions
Answer these before writing:

1. What is the subject line? (write this first — it determines everything)
2. What personal story or experience opens this issue?
3. What is the ONE thing the reader can DO after reading this?
4. What exclusive insight is in this issue that wasn't in the social posts?
5. What is the single CTA at the end?

If you can't write the subject line before you start — you don't know what the issue is about yet.

## Newsletter Templates

### Template A — Story + Framework
```
SUBJECT: [hook — specific, curiosity-driven, personal]

[OPENING — 150-200 words]
A personal story or observation that connects to the topic.
Don't explain the lesson yet. just tell the story.
End the opening with a transition: "Here's what I learned..."

[THE LESSON — 100-150 words]
The core insight in 2-3 paragraphs. direct and clear.

[THE FRAMEWORK — 400-600 words]
Break it into steps, components, or principles.
Use subheadings or numbered points for scanability.
Include at least one specific example or real number.

[TAKEAWAYS — 50-100 words]
3-5 bullet points. each one actionable.
- [action]
- [action]
- [action]

[CTA — 50 words]
One thing to do: reply, click, or try.
"If you found this useful, reply and tell me [question]."

[SIGNOFF]
[your name]
[one personal line — what you're working on, thinking about, etc.]
```

---

### Template B — Deep Dive Breakdown
```
SUBJECT: [The exact [thing] I use to [outcome]]

[OPENING — hook + why this matters to the reader]

[THE BREAKDOWN — the meat, 800-1200 words]
Walk through the full thing, step by step.
Show your work. include the messy parts.
Use headers to break it up.

[WHAT I'D DO DIFFERENTLY]
1-2 paragraphs of honest reflection.

[TAKEAWAYS]
3-5 bullets, each one actionable.

[CTA]
One clear action.
```

## Good vs Bad

### ❌ Bad subject line — generic, no reason to open
```
"Weekly Newsletter #47"
"This week's content tips"
"AI and content creation"
```

### ✅ Good subject line — specific, personal, creates curiosity
```
"The folder that replaced my content team"
"I stopped writing posts. here's what I did instead."
"The exact system: 1 topic → 8 platforms (full breakdown)"
```

### ❌ Bad opening — jumps straight to advice
```
"Today I want to share 5 tips for improving your content 
marketing strategy using AI tools..."
```

### ✅ Good opening — starts with a story
```
"Three months ago I was paying $8,000 a month for content.

A writer for LinkedIn. A different one for Instagram. 
Someone to manage the calendar. Someone to edit.

Last week I canceled all of it.

Here's what I replaced it with — and why I should have done 
it a year earlier."
```
