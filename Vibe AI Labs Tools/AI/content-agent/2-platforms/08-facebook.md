# Facebook

## Platform DNA
- Older demographic, community-driven, group-focused
- No hard character limit, sweet spot 300-800 chars
- Vibe: community, discussion. less "personal brand", more "helpful community member"
- Audience: more [[04-casual]], professionals, slightly older than X/TikTok

## Content Rules
- Questions and discussion prompts drive engagement
- Longer captions with personal context work well
- Video (native upload) gets algo priority
- Match [[02-brand-voice]] but warmer. see [[01-platform-tone]]
- Share to relevant groups, not just your profile

---

## Pre-Writing Questions
Answer these before writing:

1. What discussion do you want to start — not what do you want to say?
2. Who in the community would genuinely benefit from this?
3. Does the post end with a question that's easy to answer?
4. Is the tone warm enough? (Facebook readers are not builders on X — they want to connect, not just consume)
5. Would this feel at home in a Facebook group, not just a personal feed?

Facebook is the only platform where your goal is to START a conversation, not just deliver information.

## Post Templates

### Template A — Discussion Post (most reliable)
```
[personal observation or insight — 2-3 sentences]

[why it matters to this community — 1-2 sentences]

[specific, easy-to-answer question]
```

---

### Template B — Story + Question
```
[short personal story — 3-4 sentences]
[what you learned or what changed]

[question that connects the story to the reader's experience]
```

---

### Template C — Resource Share
```
[what you made or found — 1 sentence]
[why it's useful — 1-2 sentences]
[who specifically will find it helpful]

[question: "has anyone else tried something like this?"]
```

## Good vs Bad

### ❌ Bad — no warmth, no question, sounds like a broadcast
```
"AI is changing content marketing. Here are 5 ways to use 
AI in your content strategy. Follow for more tips. 
#AI #ContentMarketing #DigitalMarketing"
```
Why it fails: hashtags on Facebook look spammy, no question, no warmth, feels like an ad.

### ✅ Good — personal, warm, invites real replies
```
Something I've been experimenting with lately — using a folder 
of markdown files to brief AI before writing any content.

It sounds overly simple, but the output quality jumped a lot 
compared to just opening Claude and typing.

Has anyone else built a system like this? Curious what's 
working for people here.
```
Why it works: personal experience, no hype, ends with a genuine question, feels like a community member not a marketer.

## Formats That Work
1. **Discussion post** — share an insight + ask a question at the end
2. **Native video** — repurpose [[04-tiktok]] content, upload directly (not YouTube link)
3. **Personal story** — longer narrative, more vulnerable, invite comments
4. **Resource share** — "I made this, thought this community would find it useful"

---

## Posting Strategy
- Frequency: 3x/week
- Best days: Tuesday, Thursday, Saturday
- Post in relevant groups 1-2x/week
- See [[04-scheduling]]

---

## Repurposing Notes
- Expand [[01-x]] content with more personal context
- Add a question at the end to drive comments
- Soften the tone significantly — warmest version of the topic
