# TikTok

## Platform DNA
- Video platform. everything is video. 15 seconds to 10 minutes, sweet spot is 45-90 seconds
- Vibe: raw, unpolished, authentic. overproduced content actually performs WORSE here. people want real, not perfect
- Audience: youngest demographic. more [[04-casual]]. they're discovering topics, not deep-diving
- The algo is discovery-based — you dont need followers to go viral. every video gets a chance

## Content Rules
- Hook in the FIRST 2 SECONDS. if you dont grab them, they swipe. use [[01-hooks]], contrarian and discovery hooks adapted for video
- Match [[02-brand-voice]] but at its most casual and energetic. see [[01-platform-tone]]
- Talk TO the camera like you're talking to a friend
- Screen recordings with voiceover work incredibly well for tech/AI content. show, dont just tell
- No long intros. no "hey guys welcome back". jump straight in
- Captions/text overlays are mandatory. most people watch muted

---

## Pre-Writing Questions
Answer these before writing the script:

1. What is the hook — first 2 seconds, spoken out loud?
2. Can you SAY this naturally, not read it like a script?
3. Is there something to SHOW on screen? (show > tell on TikTok)
4. What is the ONE thing the viewer does or knows after watching?
5. What is the CTA — follow, comment, or try something?

If the hook doesn't grab YOU when you say it out loud — rewrite it.

## Script Templates

### Template A — Screen Recording (best for tech/tools)
```
[HOOK — 0:00-0:02]
Spoken: "[surprising claim or question]"
Text overlay: same or a shorter version

[CONTEXT — 0:02-0:07]
Spoken: "here's what I mean / here's why this matters"

[THE SHOW — 0:07-0:45]
Screen recording of you doing the thing
Voiceover explaining each step as you do it
Text overlays for key points (muted viewers)

[RESULT — 0:45-0:50]
Show the outcome. before vs after if possible.

[CTA — 0:50-0:53]
"follow for more [topic]" or "comment [word] and I'll send you [thing]"
```

---

### Template B — Quick Tip (30 seconds)
```
[HOOK — 0:00-0:02]
"most people [do X wrong]. here's the fix:"

[TIP — 0:02-0:20]
One tip, explained simply. show it if possible.

[RESULT — 0:20-0:25]
"this alone saved me [time/money/effort]"

[CTA — 0:25-0:30]
"follow for more [topic]"
```

---

### Template C — "I Replaced X With Y"
```
[HOOK — 0:00-0:02]
"I replaced [old thing] with [new thing] and here's what happened:"

[BEFORE — 0:02-0:10]
Show or describe the old painful way

[AFTER — 0:10-0:40]
Show the new way working in real time

[RESULT — 0:40-0:50]
Real numbers or outcome

[CTA — 0:50-0:55]
"comment [word] if you want the full breakdown"
```

## Good vs Bad

### ❌ Bad — long intro, stiff delivery, no hook
```
"Hey guys, welcome back to my channel! Today I'm going to be 
talking about content marketing and how AI can help you 
save time. So let's get into it..."
```
Why it fails: 8 seconds before anything interesting happens. they're already gone.

### ✅ Good — hooks in 2 seconds, jumps straight in
```
"You're still writing every post manually? Let me show you 
what I do instead." [immediately starts screen recording]
```
Why it works: creates instant curiosity, no wasted intro, visual proof follows immediately.

---

## Formats That Work
1. **The Screen Recording** — show yourself doing the thing. record your screen, add voiceover. 45-60 seconds
2. **The Quick Tip** — one actionable tip in 30 seconds. hook → tip → result
3. **The "I Replaced X With Y"** — show the transformation. before vs after
4. **The Reaction** — react to trending stuff in your niche. add your take

## Posting Strategy
- Frequency: 5-7x/week (daily if you can manage it)
- Best times: 7-9am, 12-3pm, 7-10pm
- Post consistently for 30 days before judging results
- See [[04-scheduling]]
