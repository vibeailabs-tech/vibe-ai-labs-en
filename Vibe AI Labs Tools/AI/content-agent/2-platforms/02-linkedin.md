# LinkedIn

## Platform DNA
- No hard character limit, but sweet spot is 1,300-2,000 characters for regular posts, up to 3,000 for articles
- Vibe: professional but human. personal narratives win here. "I" stories with business lessons attached
- Audience: mix of [[03-builders]] and [[04-casual]] — more professionals, decision-makers, people who actually have budgets

## Content Rules
- Lead with a personal hook. "I was wrong about..." / "3 months ago I..." / "Everyone told me to..."
- First line is EVERYTHING. LinkedIn truncates after ~210 characters with a "see more" button. if your hook isn't in that first line, nobody clicks
- Use [[01-hooks]] — proof hooks and playbook hooks perform best
- Match [[02-brand-voice]] but more professional. see [[01-platform-tone]]
- Short paragraphs. one idea per paragraph. white space is your friend
- No hashtags in the body. if you use them, max 3 at the very end
- Links in the first comment, never in the post body. algo penalizes

---

## Pre-Writing Questions
Answer these before writing a single word:

1. What personal story or experience connects to this topic?
2. What is the ONE lesson or takeaway the reader leaves with?
3. Who is this for — [[03-builders]] (tactical) or [[04-casual]] (inspirational)?
4. What hook type? — proof, playbook, personal narrative, or contrarian?
5. Does the first line work WITHOUT the rest of the post? (it must stand alone before "see more")

If you don't have a real story or real number to anchor the post — don't write it yet.

## Post Templates

### Template A — Personal Narrative (most reliable)
```
[hook: personal moment or surprising result — must fit in 210 chars]

[1-2 lines of context: what was the situation?]

[the turning point: what changed or what did you learn?]

[the breakdown: 3-5 short paragraphs, one idea each]

[the takeaway: one actionable line]

[optional: question to drive comments]
```

---

### Template B — Listicle
```
[hook: bold claim or surprising statement]

[1-2 lines of setup]

[N] things I learned about [topic]:

1. [insight] — [one line explanation]
2. [insight] — [one line explanation]
3. [insight] — [one line explanation]
...

[closing: what this means for the reader]
```

---

### Template C — Case Study
```
[hook: before → after result]

[timeframe and context: what were you doing?]

Here's exactly what changed:

[step/decision 1]
[step/decision 2]
[step/decision 3]

[what you'd do differently / what others can steal]
```

## Good vs Bad

### ❌ Bad — no story, no specifics, sounds like a press release
```
Artificial intelligence is revolutionizing content creation.
Companies that leverage AI tools will gain a significant competitive
advantage. Here are some best practices for implementing AI
in your marketing strategy. #AI #Marketing #ContentStrategy
```
Why it fails: no personal voice, no story, hashtags in body, generic advice, zero engagement trigger.

### ✅ Good — personal, specific, makes you read past "see more"
```
6 months ago I was spending $8,000 a month on content across 10 platforms.

Today I spend $0.

Not because I stopped creating. Because I built a system.

Here's exactly what changed — and what I'd do differently if starting over:
```
Why it works: specific number, personal story, curiosity gap before "see more", promises a lesson.

## Formats That Work
1. **Personal Narrative** — "I [did/learned/failed at something]" → lesson → actionable takeaway
2. **The Listicle Post** — "7 things I learned about [topic]:" with numbered items. each item 1-2 sentences
3. **The Contrarian Take** — challenge conventional wisdom. open with a bold statement, follow with reasoning
4. **The Case Study** — real results, real numbers, real process. "How we went from [before] to [after] in [timeframe]"
5. **Document/Carousel Posts** — PDF slides uploaded as a document. 10-15 slides, one idea per slide, bold headlines

## Posting Strategy
- Frequency: 3-5x/week
- Best times: 7-8am, 12pm, 5-6pm (business hours)
- Comment on 10 relevant posts before publishing yours (engagement hack)
